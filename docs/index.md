# AI Careers: Where the Work Is Growing

A research guide to emerging job titles, what people in those roles do, and practical paths into AI and adjacent technology careers.

This project treats “AI career” broadly: it includes building models, working with data, applying AI in products, and protecting the systems that use it. The roles below are established occupational categories and employer-facing titles; job titles vary across companies.

<div class="tip custom-block" style="padding: 1rem 1.25rem;">
<strong>Research snapshot</strong><br>
U.S. labor projections point to strong growth in data science, computing research, and information security. Separately, surveyed employers around the world expect AI and machine-learning specialist roles to grow quickly through 2030. These are different kinds of evidence, so compare them with care.

## Questions guiding this project

- Which job titles are genuinely new, and which are familiar occupations changing with AI?
- What does a normal week look like for someone in each role?
- Which skills transfer between software, data, research, and security careers?
- What entry routes exist for students, including degrees, projects, and internships?
- How do regional demand, pay, and education requirements vary?

## How to read the outlook

Growth projections describe groups of occupations over a stated period. They do not guarantee that an individual will find a job, and they may not map perfectly onto newer titles such as “prompt engineer.” This project uses established occupational data as a baseline and keeps employer survey expectations separate.
