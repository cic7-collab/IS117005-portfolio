# Sources

## Labor-market outlook

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, [Data Scientists: Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm). U.S. employment outlook, 2024–34; occupation description and typical preparation.
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, [Computer and Information Research Scientists: Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-and-information-research-scientists.htm). U.S. employment outlook, 2024–34; duties and education.
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, [Information Security Analysts: Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/information-security-analysts.htm). U.S. employment outlook, 2024–34; duties and security demand factors.
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, [Software Developers: Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm). U.S. employment outlook, 2024–34.
- World Economic Forum, [The Future of Jobs Report 2025: Jobs Outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook). Employer expectations for role growth through 2030 across the surveyed organizations.
