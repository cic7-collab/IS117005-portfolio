# Career profiles

These profiles are a starting point for deeper research. The job descriptions are concise summaries, not copied job postings. Employers use overlapping titles, so compare responsibilities and required skills in actual listings.

## AI / Machine Learning Engineer

**Core purpose:** Turn machine-learning methods into reliable software features or services.

**Typical work:** Prepare data; train, fine-tune, or integrate models; evaluate quality; build inference pipelines; monitor performance and cost; collaborate with product and infrastructure teams.

**Useful skills:** Python, software engineering, model evaluation, data handling, APIs, cloud or deployment fundamentals, and clear documentation. Production roles often require more than prompt writing: they involve testing, reliability, privacy, and ongoing maintenance.

**Possible routes:** Computer science, data science, statistics, or engineering study; then projects that show an end-to-end system with evaluation and documentation. Some people enter through software engineering or data engineering and specialize over time.

**Outlook evidence:** The World Economic Forum names AI and machine-learning specialists among the fastest-growing roles in its 2025 employer survey for 2025–30. This is a global employer expectation, not a U.S. government count of one standardized occupation. [WEF report](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook)

## Data Scientist

**Core purpose:** Use data to answer questions, explain patterns, and support decisions or predictions.

**Typical work:** Collect and clean data; analyze it with statistics and code; build and validate models; communicate findings to technical and non-technical partners.

**Useful skills:** Statistics, Python or R, SQL, visualization, experimental reasoning, and communication. Subject knowledge matters because a model is only useful when the question and data make sense.

**Possible routes:** BLS says a bachelor's degree in mathematics, statistics, computer science, or a related field is typical; some employers prefer graduate study. Portfolio projects can demonstrate analysis, responsible interpretation, and communication.

**Outlook evidence:** BLS projects 34% U.S. employment growth from 2024 to 2034, with about 23,400 openings per year on average. The projection covers the standardized data scientist occupation, not every data or AI title. [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm)

## Computer and Information Research Scientist

**Core purpose:** Create or improve computing methods, including algorithms and AI techniques.

**Typical work:** Design experiments; develop theory or prototypes; test algorithms; publish or present findings; collaborate with teams in industry, government, or academia.

**Useful skills:** Advanced programming, algorithms, mathematics, research design, and technical writing. Depending on the specialty, graduate-level study may be common.

**Possible routes:** BLS reports that a master's degree is typically needed for entry. Research experience, strong foundations in computer science and math, and a clear specialization can help prepare for this path.

**Outlook evidence:** BLS projects 20% U.S. employment growth from 2024 to 2034 and about 3,200 openings annually on average. [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-and-information-research-scientists.htm)

## Information Security Analyst

**Core purpose:** Protect an organization's networks, systems, and information from security threats.

**Typical work:** Monitor for incidents; investigate weaknesses; maintain protective measures; help plan responses; assess risks in new systems and technology.

**Useful skills:** Networking, operating systems, security fundamentals, scripting, careful investigation, and communication. Knowledge of AI systems can help analysts assess new attack surfaces and misuse risks.

**Possible routes:** BLS says a bachelor's degree in a computer science field plus related work experience is typical; certifications may be preferred. IT support, networking, and system administration can provide stepping stones.

**Outlook evidence:** BLS projects 29% U.S. employment growth from 2024 to 2034, with about 16,000 openings annually on average. BLS cites greater cybersecurity needs, including securing new technologies, as a factor. [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/information-security-analysts.htm)

## Software Developer

**Core purpose:** Design, build, and maintain software applications and systems, including products that use AI services.

**Typical work:** Translate user needs into software; write and review code; test features; work with databases, APIs, and deployment systems; maintain software after release.

**Useful skills:** Programming, data structures, debugging, version control, testing, teamwork, and system design. AI product development also benefits from understanding model limitations, evaluation, privacy, and human review.

**Possible routes:** Computer science degree, software-focused coursework, or another route supported by substantial projects and practical experience. A strong portfolio should explain the problem, design decisions, testing, and the developer's own contribution.

**Outlook evidence:** The World Economic Forum lists software and applications developers among roles expected to grow through 2030. BLS projects 16% growth for software developers in the United States from 2024 to 2034. [WEF report](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook) · [BLS projections](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm)

## Big Data Specialist

**Core purpose:** Build or manage systems that collect, organize, and process very large datasets.

**Typical work:** Create data pipelines; manage distributed storage and processing; make reliable, high-quality data available for analytics and AI teams.

**Useful skills:** SQL, programming, databases, cloud platforms, distributed systems, data modeling, and data quality practices.

**Possible routes:** Data engineering, computer science, information systems, or analytics training combined with projects that process and validate real datasets.

**Outlook evidence:** Big Data Specialists rank among the fastest-growing roles in the WEF's global employer survey for 2025–30. This title does not map one-to-one to a single U.S. BLS occupation, so the survey result should not be read as a U.S. employment projection. [WEF report](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook)

## A practical comparison checklist

When researching a title, record the employer, location, date posted, responsibilities, minimum qualifications, preferred qualifications, pay range if listed, and whether AI is central to the work or one tool among many. Collect multiple listings before drawing conclusions.
